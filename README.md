# The-Wordy-Wizard
Games to help kids learn to spell

## For teachers

1. Tap **Grown-ups**, solve the math problem, and enter the week's spelling words.
2. Type a class name if you like (for example "Room 12") and press **Create a classroom code**.
3. Give the children the 4-digit code. They tap **Join my class** in the wizard's room, type the code, and check it's their class.

Each time you press **Save words**, the class gets the new list.

## The Royal Map

A crossword made from the week's words: each word is a road between two villages on the kingdom map. The clues on the Royal Scroll are written once for each word and read aloud; tap **Hear the word** if you get stuck. Finish every road and the king's banner goes up over the castle. Grown-ups can rewrite any clue: tap 🔊 next to the word in Grown-ups.

## The Wizard's Tale

The first spell in the book. A short story made from the week's spelling words is read aloud in the same clear voice as the games, with the words in bold lighting up as they're said. Children just listen; they can pause, start again, or tap a sentence to hear it. Each word list gets one story, shared by everyone using that list, and it's readied as soon as the words are saved.
